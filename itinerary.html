DAY 3 Sun, Oct 11
🏮⛩️
🗼 Tokyo
Meiji Shrine, Tokyo Tower & Ginza
Full day tour of Tokyo, home to over 11 million people. Visit Meiji Shrine, a key Shinto site dedicated to the Imperial Family in 1926 and one of Tokyo's most popular shrines. Then see Tokyo Tower (332.9m, built in 1958, the second-tallest structure in Japan). In the afternoon, explore Ginza — Tokyo's most famous upmarket shopping, dining, and entertainment district.

DAY 4 Mon, Oct 12
🐟🤖
🗼 Tokyo
Tsukiji Market, Odaiba & Sensoji Temple
Start at Tsukiji Outer Market — fresh seafood, local delicacies, and authentic street food. Then explore futuristic Odaiba, home to the life-sized Gundam statue. Finally, step back in time at Asakusa's Sensoji Temple, Tokyo's oldest and most iconic temple, and stroll Nakamise Street's traditional shops.
🍳 Breakfast
🐟 🤖 ⛩️ 🍡
DAY 5 Tue, Oct 13
🗻🏞️
🗻 Tokyo → Mt. Fuji → Shizuoka/Aichi
Mt. Fuji, Lake Kawaguchi & Oshino Hakkai
Check out of the hotel and board the tour bus for a full day of sightseeing at the UNESCO World Heritage Mt. Fuji area. Visit Lake Kawaguchi, a crater lake formed by a volcanic eruption nearly 3,000 years ago with postcard views of Mt. Fuji. Then Oshino Hakkai ("Eight Seas of Oshino") — eight spring-fed ponds, part of the Mt. Fuji UNESCO World Cultural Heritage site. Drive to Shizuoka/Aichi for the night.

⛩️ Shizuoka/Aichi → Kyoto
Travel to Kyoto & Fushimi Inari Shrine
Depart Shizuoka/Aichi for Kyoto, Japan's cultural and artistic center and the country's capital for over 1,000 years. Visit the iconic Fushimi Inari Shrine, renowned for its thousands of vermilion torii gates. The rest of the day is yours to explore Kyoto at your leisure.

⛩️ Kyoto
Kinkaku-ji, Arashiyama Bamboo Forest & Sanjusangendo
In-depth exploration of Kyoto's most iconic landmarks: the golden splendor of Kinkaku-ji Temple, the serene Arashiyama Bamboo Forest, and the awe-inspiring Sanjusangendo — home to 1,001 intricately crafted statues of Kannon, the Goddess of Mercy.

DAY 9 Sat, Oct 17
🦌🏯
🦌 Kyoto → Nara → 🏯 Osaka
Todaiji in Nara Deer Park, Dotonbori & Osaka Castle
Visit Tōdaiji in Nara Deer Park, the "Great Eastern Temple" and one of Japan's most historically significant temples. Then on to Osaka — first stop Dotonbori, a vibrant commercial street since the Edo Period, with free time for lunch and shopping. Finally, visit Osaka Castle. Overnight stay near KIX Airport.
