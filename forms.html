Forms & Reservation Steps

What you need to submit and when
📋 Reservation Checklist
  • 1. Intake Form — complete and submit to your travel advisor
  • 2. Traveler's Passport — first page copy for each traveler
  • 3. Initial Deposit — to secure your spot
  • 4. Final Payment — full payment due by 90 days prior to departure date

Cancellation policy: All deposits are non-refundable and non-transferable. All final balances are non-refundable and non-transferable. Travel insurance is highly recommended — you will be asked to provide your insurance info around 30 days prior to departure.
