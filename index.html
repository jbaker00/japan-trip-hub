City by City, Day by Day
Hopping between cities by Shinkansen (bullet train) 🚄
DAY 1 Fri, Oct 9
🧳✈️
✈️ In Transit
Depart Home & Fly to Japan
Say your goodbyes and head to the airport! We'll cross the International Date Line en route — buckle up, snack up, and get ready to land in Japan tomorrow.
🧳 ✈️ 🎧 😴
DAY 2 Sat, Oct 10
🛬🌸
🗼 Tokyo
Arrival & Welcome Night
Land in Tokyo, settle into our hotel, and meet up for a welcome dinner with the group. First taste of ramen, first round of jet lag — the adventure begins!
🛬 🏨 🍜 🎉
DAY 3 Sun, Oct 11
🏮⛩️
🗼 Tokyo
Shibuya, Asakusa & Senso-ji Temple
Cross the famous Shibuya Scramble, wander Asakusa's Nakamise shopping street, and visit Tokyo's oldest temple, Senso-ji. Evening free for izakaya hopping.
⛩️ 🛍️ 🏮 📸
DAY 4 Mon, Oct 12
🗼🍡
🗼 Tokyo
Tokyo Tower & Team Free Day
Sunset views from Tokyo Tower, then an open afternoon to explore Harajuku, Akihabara, or just relax before we head out of the city tomorrow.
🗼 🌇 🎮 🍡
DAY 5 Tue, Oct 13
🚄🦊
⛩️ Kyoto
Shinkansen to Kyoto + Fushimi Inari
Bullet train from Tokyo to Kyoto (~2.5 hrs). Afternoon exploring the thousand torii gates of Fushimi Inari Shrine.
⛩️ 🦊 🌿 🧧
🚄 Tokyo → Kyoto by Shinkansen
DAY 6 Wed, Oct 14
🏯🦌
🦌 Nara (day trip)
Kinkaku-ji & Nara Deer Park
Morning at the Golden Pavilion (Kinkaku-ji), then a short train ride to Nara to feed the famously polite bowing deer.
🏯 🦌 🍘 🌳
🚄 Kyoto → Nara (local train)
DAY 7 Thu, Oct 15
🏯🐙
🏯 Osaka
Osaka Castle & Dotonbori Nights
Short train hop to Osaka. Explore the moated Osaka Castle by day, then dive into the neon chaos of Dotonbori — street food capital of Japan.
🏯 🐙 🍢 💡
🚄 Kyoto → Osaka by Shinkansen
DAY 8 Fri, Oct 16
🕊️⛩️
🕊️ Hiroshima & Miyajima (day trip)
Peace Memorial & Floating Torii Gate
Day trip to Hiroshima's Peace Memorial Park, followed by a ferry to Miyajima Island to see the iconic floating torii gate at Itsukushima Shrine.
🕊️ ⛩️ 🦌 🛳️
🚄 Osaka → Hiroshima by Shinkansen
DAY 9 Sat, Oct 17
🗻♨️
🗻 Hakone
Mt. Fuji Views & Onsen Soak
Travel to Hakone for hot spring relaxation and (weather permitting) stunning views of Mt. Fuji across Lake Ashi.
🗻 ♨️ 🚡 🍶
🚄 Hiroshima → Hakone via Shinkansen
DAY 10 Sun, Oct 18
🥢💕
🗼 Tokyo
Back to Tokyo & Sayonara, Japan!
Return to Tokyo for last-minute souvenir shopping, a farewell group meal, then transfer to the airport and head home with a suitcase full of memories (and probably too many snacks).
🛍️ 🎁 🍶 🥢
🚄 Hakone → Tokyo by train
