Travel Protection / Insurance

Coverage options and what to know before you go
🛡️ Trip Insurance
  • Health Insurance — check with your healthcare insurance provider to understand your coverage outside your home country
  • Card Travel Coverage — check with your credit card company (the card used for hotel and flight reservations) to understand travel coverages
  • Trip Insurance — purchase trip coverage insurance if you want to be covered for inconveniences that might cause trip interruption or cancellation. On average, comprehensive trip insurance runs around 10–15% of trip cost.

Compare plans:

  • insuremytrip.com (compare plans)
  • travelexinsurance.com (has option to be primary)
  • travelcorners.com (has option to be primary)
  • bhtp.com — Berkshire Hathaway Travel Protection (has option to be primary)
  • travelguard.com

Or contact your Travel Advisor, Chantale, directly for help comparing options.

⏰ Timing Reminder

Travel insurance is highly recommended. You will be asked to provide your insurance info around 30 days prior to departure.
